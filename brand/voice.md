# Brand voice guide
- Brand personality: direct, technical, empowering, developer-first
- Three voice pillars: Technical Precision, Empowering, Direct
- 10 ON-brand vs OFF-brand examples:
  1. Install: ON: "pip install profileforge" vs OFF: "Simply download our amazing tool!"
  2. Error: ON: "Widget 'github_stats' failed: GITHUB_TOKEN not set." vs OFF: "Oops! Something went wrong."
  3. Feature: ON: "15 production-grade widgets. Zero string concatenation." vs OFF: "Many exciting customization options!"
  4. CTA: ON: "Star the repo. Ship your profile." vs OFF: "Please consider giving us a star if you like it!"
  5. Onboarding: ON: "3 commands. Your profile is live." vs OFF: "Get started on your amazing journey!"
  6. Performance: ON: "Sub-15ms end-to-end. Verified on every commit." vs OFF: "Super fast performance you'll love!"
  7. Community: ON: "PRs welcome. Read CONTRIBUTING.md first." vs OFF: "We'd love for you to join our family!"
  8. Themes: ON: "Drop a YAML file. Extend any base theme." vs OFF: "Customize your look with our powerful theme creator!"
  9. Error handling: ON: "render_safe() isolates failures. One broken widget never crashes your profile." vs OFF: "Don't worry, we handle errors for you!"
  10. Docs: ON: "Required: Python 3.9+, pip, a GitHub token for live data widgets." vs OFF: "Make sure you have the necessary prerequisites installed!"
- Words to use: forge, build, ship, declarative, compose, generate, render, precise, production-grade
- Words to avoid: simply, easy, amazing, journey, powerful, seamless, intuitive, magic
