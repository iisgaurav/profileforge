# The Maintainer Pathway

We believe in a transparent and open path to leadership within the ProfileForge ecosystem. If you are passionate about the project, here is how you can grow from a contributor to a core maintainer.

## Stage 1: Contributor

**Criteria:**
- 5+ merged Pull Requests.
- Responsive to feedback.
- Follows the community guidelines and technical standards.

**What you do:**
- Write code, fix bugs, add documentation.

## Stage 2: Reviewer

**Criteria:**
- Invited by current maintainers after showing consistent quality in PRs.
- Deep understanding of the codebase and architecture.

**What you do:**
- Review incoming PRs from the community.
- Triage issues (add labels, reproduce bugs, request information).
- You do not have merge rights yet, but your `LGTM` (Looks Good To Me) acts as a strong signal.

## Stage 3: Maintainer

**Criteria:**
- Demonstrated long-term commitment.
- Excellent technical judgment and empathy towards contributors.
- Endorsed by existing maintainers.

**What you do:**
- Merge PRs.
- Cut releases and publish to PyPI.
- Shape the engineering and product roadmap.
- Act as a community moderator.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

As a maintainer, you are expected to uphold the following standards:
- **Review SLA:** Aim to review PRs within 3-5 business days.
- **Values:** Uphold technical excellence, inclusivity, and long-term architectural thinking.
- **Mentorship:** Guide first-time contributors patiently.

## How to Express Interest

If you want to step up to a Reviewer or Maintainer role, let us know!
Open a thread in the **GitHub Discussions** under the `Community` category, or ping an existing maintainer in an issue. We are always looking for help!
